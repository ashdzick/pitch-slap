LinkedIn Bingo/Tic‑Tac‑Toe

How to play
  1. Make a card. Tap any square to rewrite it, or Shuffle for new ones.
  2. Claim a square you've actually seen (or done) on LinkedIn. You're ✕.
  3. Instead of pitch slapping a recent connection, send them the link. They claim a square as ◯ and send a link back.
  4. Keep trading links in the same DM. First to three in a row wins.

Based on the original LinkedIn Bingo/Tic‑Tac‑Toe graphic, made in collaboration with Marya Jan. No tracking; games live in your browser and your links.
